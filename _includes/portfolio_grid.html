Our solution: Biosphera

Biopshera is Subterra’s GeoBiological Software to de-risk industry investments in Natural Hydrogen prospecting and Underground Hydrogen storage

Biosphera is Subterra’s revolutionary web application—designed to bring the invisible world of subsurface microbiology into the hands of energy professionals. Built on cutting-edge science and proprietary data, Biosphera allows clients to assess the presence and impact of microbial life in geological reservoirs. It models the expected size, diversity, and activity of microbial communities, simulates hydrogen consumption under real-world conditions, and calculates risk indexes to guide + exploration, storage, and operational decisions. Integrated with leading geochemical and geological software, and powered by AI-assisted analysis, Biosphera turns complex biological data into clear, actionable insights. Whether you're developing a natural hydrogen prospect, planning underground hydrogen storage, or managing a carbon capture or gas field project, Biosphera helps you anticipate microbial risks before they become costly problems—transforming microbiology from a blind spot into a strategic advantage.
