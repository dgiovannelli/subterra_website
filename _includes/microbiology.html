Why Hydrogen?

Natural hydrogen is a clean, renewable energy source generated continuously by geological processes in the Earth’s crust. Unlike manufactured hydrogen, it requires no carbon-intensive production and can be tapped directly from subsurface reservoirs. Unlocking natural hydrogen could transform the global energy landscape—delivering low-cost, low-emission energy at scale.

Why Microbiology?

The subsurface is alive. Microbial life thrives kilometers beneath the surface and plays an active role in the chemistry and behavior of underground reservoirs. In hydrogen storage and exploration, these invisible ecosystems can consume hydrogen, alter reservoir properties, produce corrosive byproducts like hydrogen sulfide, and jeopardize caprock integrity. Ignoring microbiology means ignoring a key risk factor that can compromise safety, reduce recoverable hydrogen, and inflate costs.
